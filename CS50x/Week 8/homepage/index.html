Chaos game theory

The chaos game is a mathematical method that uses random steps to create structured, repeating patterns called fractals.
By repeatedly plotting points stopping some distance (called the ratio) toward a randomly chosen corner of a shape, a fractal will emerge. The most famous of these is the Sierpinski triangle. It is achieved by plotting a point inside a triangle and traveling halfway each time to a randomly selected vertex of the triangle.
