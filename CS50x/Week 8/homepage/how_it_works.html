  • Fractal Generator online
  1. Draw a shape.
  2. Pick a start point. It can be anywhere, even outside the shape.
  3. Roll a die equal to the number of points of your shape. (A virtual die is best here.)
  4. Travel the distance (ratio) to the chosen point. For example, halfway for ratio 0.5.
  5. Plot a point and start over.
  6. Do this 50,000 times and voilà. You have a fractal.
